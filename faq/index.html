Become an ambassador

The basics

What is a Sttar ambassador?

Someone who shares their own Sttar invite link. When a new friend joins from it and starts posting, the ambassador gets paid.

Who can join?

Anyone with a Sttar account. You do not need followers. Most friends join from group chats.

What is Sttar?

Up to five photos a day with your people. Friends’ Daily Shots come newest first, with no algorithm, and your days build into an archive. sttar.com

Friends and counting

When does a friend count?

When they make a new Sttar account from your link, follow you first within 2 days, and post 3 Daily Shots in their first week.

My friend joined but is not showing up

They need to follow you before anyone else. Your link opens your profile to make that easy. New friends appear on your stats page within an hour.

Does someone who already has Sttar count?

No. Only accounts made after you got your link.

Getting paid

How much do I earn?

$2 for each friend who qualifies, up to $50 a month. Groups earn $2 per friend for their cause, up to $200 a month.

When and how do I get paid?

Once a month by Venmo or Zelle, for friends who qualified the month before. Add where to send it on your stats page.

What about taxes?

You are responsible for taxes on what you earn. If you earn $600 or more in a year in the US, we may ask for tax details.

Groups

How do group links work?

Your group signs up once and gets one link for everyone. A friend counts for the group when they join and follow your group’s Sttar account first. More on groups

Can I have a personal link and be in a group?

Yes, as long as the group uses a different Sttar account, like a chapter account.

Your account

How do I get back to my stats?

Tap My stats at the top and sign in with your Sttar username and its email.

It says my account uses Hide My Email

If you joined Sttar with Apple’s Hide My Email, we cannot check your email here. Write to hello@sttar.com and we will set you up.

Can I post my link publicly?

Yes. Say you are a Sttar ambassador when you do. See the program rules.
