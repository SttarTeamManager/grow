THE BASICS

What is a Sttar ambassador?

Someone who shares their own Sttar invite link with friends. When a new friend joins from it and starts posting, the ambassador gets paid.

Who can join?

Anyone with a Sttar account. No follower count needed. Most friends join from group chats and direct messages.

What's Sttar?

A social archive: up to five photos a day with your people, your friends' Daily Shots newest first with no algorithm, and an archive of your days that builds itself. sttar.com

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining is free and so is Sttar.

FRIENDS AND COUNTING

When does a friend count?

When they make a brand-new Sttar account from your link, follow you first within 2 days, and post 3 Daily Shots in their first week.

My friend joined but isn't showing up

They need to follow you before anyone else. Your link opens your profile to make that easy. New friends appear on your stats page within an hour.

Does someone who already has Sttar count?

No. Only accounts made after you got your link.

What's a Daily Shot?

A photo posted to their Sttar of the Day. They can post up to 5 a day, and any 3 in their first week count.

How fast do stats update?

Every hour. Link visits update right away.

GETTING PAID

How much do I earn?

$2 for each friend who qualifies, up to $50 a month. Groups earn $2 per friend for their cause, up to $200 a month.

When and how do I get paid?

Once a month by Venmo or Zelle, for friends who qualified the month before. Add where to send it on your stats page.

Do I need to do anything for taxes?

You're responsible for taxes on what you earn. If you earn $600 or more in a year in the US, we may ask for tax details.

GROUPS

How do group links work?

Your sorority, frat or club signs up once and gets one link for everyone to share. A friend counts for the group when they join and follow your group's Sttar account first. More about groups

Where does a group's money go?

To the cause you pick when you sign up, like your philanthropy. We send it to the Venmo or Zelle you add on your group's stats page.

Can I have a personal link and be in a group?

Yes, as long as the group uses a different Sttar account than yours, like a chapter account.

YOUR ACCOUNT

How do I get back to my stats?

Tap My stats at the top and sign in with your Sttar username and its email.

It says my account uses Hide My Email

If you signed up to Sttar with Apple's Hide My Email, we can't check your email here. Message hello@sttar.com and we'll set you up.

Can I post my link publicly?

Yes. Just mention you're a Sttar ambassador when you do. See the program rules.
