Questions

My friend joined but isn't showing up

They need to follow you before anyone else, within 2 days of signing up. Your link opens your profile so they can do it right away. New friends appear here within an hour.

Does someone who already has Sttar count?

No. Only brand-new accounts made after you got your link count.

What's a Daily Shot?

A photo posted to their Sttar of the Day. Up to 5 a day, and any 3 in their first week qualifies them.

When do I get paid?

Once a month, for everyone who qualified the month before. Add your Venmo or Zelle above so we know where to send it.

Where should I share my link?

Group chats and direct messages work best. Friends who join together post more, so invite a few at once.
