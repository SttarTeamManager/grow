Program rules

The short version of how grow.sttar.com works.

What counts

  1. The friend makes a brand-new Sttar account after you got your link.
  2. You're the first person they follow, within 2 days of signing up.
  3. They post 3 Daily Shots in their first week.

Getting paid

  1. You earn $2 for each friend who qualifies, up to $50 in a calendar month.
  2. We pay once a month by Venmo or Zelle, for friends who qualified the month before.
  3. You're responsible for any taxes on what you earn. If you earn $600 or more in a year in the US, we may need tax details from you.

What's not allowed

  1. Fake, duplicate or second accounts, including your own.
  2. Paying or pressuring people to sign up, or spamming your link.
  3. Saying things about Sttar that aren't true.
  4. Hiding that you're part of this program when you post your link publicly.

The fine print

  1. You need your own Sttar account to take part.
  2. We can remove anyone, or hold back payment, if we see abuse of these rules.
  3. We may change or end the program. Friends who already qualified still count.
