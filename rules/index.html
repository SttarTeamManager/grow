program rules.

The short version of how Sttar ambassadors works.

What counts

  • The friend makes a new Sttar account after you got your link.
  • They follow your account, or your group’s account, first, within 2 days of signing up.
  • They post 3 Daily Shots in their first week.

Getting paid

  • People earn $2 per friend who qualifies, up to $50 a calendar month.
  • Groups earn $2 per friend for their cause, up to $200 a calendar month.
  • We pay once a month by Venmo or Zelle, for friends who qualified the month before.
  • You are responsible for any taxes on what you earn. If you earn $600 or more in a year in the US, we may need tax details from you.

Not allowed

  • Fake, duplicate or second accounts, including your own.
  • Paying or pressuring people to sign up, or spamming your link.
  • Saying things about Sttar that are not true.
  • Hiding that you are part of this program when you post your link publicly.

Fine print

  • You need your own Sttar account to take part. Groups need a Sttar account for their link.
  • Your username, or your group’s name, can appear on the public leaderboard.
  • We can remove anyone, or hold back payment, if we see these rules broken.
  • We may change or end the program. Friends who already qualified still count.
