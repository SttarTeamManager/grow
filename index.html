Questions

quick answers

Do I need a lot of followers?

No. Most friends join from group chats and direct messages. If you have friends who'd use Sttar, you can do this.

Who counts as a friend?

Someone who makes a brand-new Sttar account from your link, follows you first, and posts 3 Daily Shots in their first week.

How do I get paid?

Once a month by Venmo or Zelle. You add where to send it on your stats page.

Can my sorority do this together?

Yes. Sign up as a group and your chapter gets one link. Earnings go to the cause you pick, like your philanthropy.
