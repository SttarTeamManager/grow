Questions

group questions

Why does the link need a Sttar account?

That's how we track it. The link opens that account's profile, and a new friend counts for your group when they follow it first. A chapter account keeps it separate from anyone's personal profile.

Can members also have their own personal link?

Yes, with their own personal account. Each Sttar account can be either a personal ambassador or a group's account, not both.

Who gets the money?

Your cause. You tell us where it goes when you sign up, and add the Venmo or Zelle we should send it to on your group's stats page.

Who can see our stats?

Anyone with your group's stats link. Share it with your exec board or the whole chapter.
