how a group link works

For sororities, frats, clubs and teams.

  1. Sign up once

    Your group’s name, the cause the money goes to, and a Sttar account for the link to open.

  2. Everyone shares it

    Post it in the GroupMe and on stories. New friends follow your group’s account.

  3. The money adds up

    Each new friend who posts 3 Daily Shots in a week adds $2, paid monthly to your cause.

what you need to start

A group name

group questions

All questions

Why does the link need a Sttar account?

That is how we track it. The link opens that profile, and a new friend counts for your group when they follow it first.

Can members have their own link too?

Yes, with their personal accounts. Each Sttar account is either a person or a group, never both.

Who can see our stats?

Anyone you send your group’s stats link to. Share it with your exec board or the whole chapter.
